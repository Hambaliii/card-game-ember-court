EMBER COURT is an original dueling card battler that runs entirely in the browser. You face the Ashen Magistrate across a candle-lit court table: both duelists start at 30 life, draw from their own decks, and spend mana that grows from 1 to 10 across the duel. Cards belong to four suits, each with its own colour and shape — Ember (red flame), Tide (blue droplet), Thorn (green leaf) and Gale (gold swirl) — and every card is drawn as shaped vector art with its cost, attack and health on its face. Play minions and spells from a hand of five, watch damage float off the table, and bring the Magistrate to zero before he does the same to you. The game is deterministic for a given seed, with animated card flight, hover and selection states, turn banners, draw and discard piles, a scrolling duel log, and win/loss end screens with their own jingles — all in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with WebAudio sound, no build step and no dependencies.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a browser to play. (`node main.ts` regenerates the entry dossier instead of serving the game.)
